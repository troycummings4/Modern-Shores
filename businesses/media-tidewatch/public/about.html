About Tidewatch

Tidewatch is a weekly newsletter and site about coastal living, ocean life, and the people who work the water — fishing towns, lighthouse keepers, surfers, sailors, and everyone whose life runs on tide charts.

We publish one well-reported piece a week rather than a high-volume feed. The business model is subscriber-supported: a free weekly edition for everyone, funded long-term by a paid tier with deeper reporting and an ad-free archive (not yet built in this demo — see the README for what a real paid-tier build-out would need).
